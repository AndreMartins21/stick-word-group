1How the bot works

The bot is a member of the class group. It stays out of the conversation: it only reads what is meant for it.

What the bot reads in the group

✅ It reads

  • Messages that tag the bot (type @ and pick the bot's contact).
  • Replies (quotes) to a bot message: they count as tagging it.
  • Commands starting with ! or /, like !list or /list.
  • Voice notes that reply to (quote) a bot message.

🙈 It ignores

  • The class's normal conversation: the bot doesn't read it, store it or even mark it as read.
  • Photos, stickers, videos and audio that don't reply to the bot.
  • Tagging or quoting someone else.

Which language it speaks

The bot replies in English, in short messages, to keep the immersion. In beginner classes (A1-A2 and A2-B1), each word also comes with the Portuguese translation (the 🇧🇷 line). From B1-B2 up, nothing appears in Portuguese.

What about private chat?

Studying happens in the group. In private chat, the bot only serves administrators, and only with the admin commands (section 10). If a student messages it privately, the bot doesn't reply. It's not a bug, it's on purpose.

The three activities

📚 Vocabularyany time: someone tags the bot with a word and the class practices
🔁 DailyMon to Fri, ~19:00: 5 of the class's words, anyone can answer
🏆 WeeklyFriday, ~13:00: one question per student, with a tag

Answers in the daily review and in the weekly challenge earn points in the engagement ranking (section 7).

Example: the bot ignores the chat and only replies to whoever tags it

Anahey, did anyone watch yesterday's class?
Brunoyes! the teacher talked about "stall"
Bruno@bot stall | the talks stalled

The first two messages are class conversation: the bot doesn't read them. The third one tags the bot.

The word "card"

The bot replies with a card: part of speech and level (CEFR), definition, a memory tip and an example sentence. Right after that, it sends two voice notes with the pronunciation: the word and the example sentence.

3Practicing a word

After the card, the word stays "open". Anyone in the class can write a sentence with it or pick a menu option, always tagging the bot.

You sendThe bot does

@bot 2 (or !2)3 more example sentences, without repeating the ones already shown.
@bot 3 (or !3)Synonyms, each with a meaning and an example.
@bot 4 (or !4)Saves and closes the word, and suggests related expressions.
@bot 5 (or !5)Discards the word just created. If someone already wrote a sentence with it, it stays on the list.

4List, practice and removal

Everything the class adds becomes a numbered list, the same for everyone in the group.

Each word has a fixed number (1 is the oldest). Use !list 2 for the next page. In classes from B1-B2 up, the list shows the English definition instead of the translation.

!practice: practice again

  • !practice on its own: opens the oldest pending (not yet practiced) word.

!delete: remove a word from the list

  • !delete stall or !delete 23.
  • Only whoever added the word, or a bot admin, can delete it. Everyone else gets a notice.

5Daily review

Every weekday, around 19:00, the bot brings words from the class list to review. Nobody gets tagged: it's an invitation for whoever is around.

  1. The bot opens the round with 5 words picked by spaced repetition: the ones that are "due" come first.
  2. For each word, anyone answers by tagging the bot: explains the word in English in their own words or writes a sentence with it.
  3. The AI gives feedback. The first acceptable answer takes the word, and the bot sends the next one.
  4. If the answer isn't good yet, the bot gives a hint and the word stays open for someone else to try.
  5. At the end, the bot sends the summary: the solid words, the ones coming back soon and the skipped ones.

Example round

bot⏰ Practice time — 5 words to review.
bot🔁 1/5 · stall Explain it in English in your own words, or write a sentence using it (tag me to answer). Anyone can answer · tag me with skip for the next word · skipall to stop the review
Bruno@bot it's when you stop
bot💬 Bruno answered: ❌ Close, but too vague: stall is about progress stopping. 💬 Try: "It means to stop making progress." The word is still open — anyone can try again, or !skip.
Ana@bot My English stalled when I stopped watching series
bot👏 Ana answered: ✅ Great sentence! "Stalled" shows your progress stopped. 🔁 2/5 · no cap ...

End of the round

bot🎉 Practice done — 5 reviewed. ✅ Solid: stall, no cap, drag on 🔁 Coming back soon: grind to a halt ⏭️ Skipped: breakthrough Add a new word whenever you want: tag me with a word.

How the bot decides when each word comes back

Everyone remembers words at their own pace. That's why the bot keeps a class memory: for each student and each word, it knows whether that person got it right or wrong in the daily review, and how long ago. As the days go by, the chance of remembering drops.

  • A word only starts coming back later once almost the whole class has shown they remember it, not just whoever answered fastest.
  • Wrong answers count too: if several people get it wrong, the word comes back sooner.
  • Only people who take part in the daily review count. Someone who only reads the group doesn't hold the class's words back.

This is spaced repetition (the idea behind Anki), adapted for a class: what the class already knows shows up less and less, and what someone still forgets comes back soon.

Commands during the review

CommandWhat it does

Automatic rules

  • The time can vary by up to 20 minutes earlier or later.
  • The round closes by itself after 3 hours without any message in the group.
  • After 3 reviews in a row with no answers, the bot pauses the daily review. Any message from the class turns it back on.
  • If the class has no words yet, the bot stays silent.

6Weekly challenge

Once a week (default: Friday, around 13:00), the bot creates open questions with the class's vocabulary, at the class's level, and tags one student per question.

  1. The AI picks up to 15 words from the list, favoring the ones the class forgets the most (from the class memory, section 5).
  2. It creates one question per student (up to 10). Teachers and admins are never tagged.
  3. Each question starts by tagging that turn's student, who gets the question with the words they most need to review. Students take turns from one week to the next.
  4. Anyone can try and gets feedback, but the question only moves on when the tagged student answers or someone skips.
  5. The answer has to make sense for the question. Using the class's words isn't required, but it makes the answer better.

Rules

  • The challenge only reads the class memory: its answers don't change when words come back in the daily review. They count toward the participation ranking.
  • It closes by itself after 3 hours without any message in the group.
  • If the daily review and the challenge fall at the same time, the challenge comes first.
  • The time can vary by up to 20 minutes earlier or later.
  • !weekly shows the day, the time and the next challenge.

🛠️ Admins only: !weekly mon 9h (day and time), !weekly size 3 (number of questions; size auto goes back to one per student), !weekly off / !weekly on and !weekly now (starts the challenge right away).

7Engagement ranking

The ranking shows who took part the most. Admins get the ranking as a PDF in private chat with /ranking.

What earns points

✅ Counts

  • Each answer in the daily review.
  • Each answer in the weekly challenge, even if you're not the tagged student.
  • Every attempt counts, including the ones that got ❌.

❌ Doesn't count

  • Adding words.
  • Practicing a word outside the review (menu 2-5, free sentences).
  • Teachers, admins and the owner: they stay out of the ranking.

How the order works

RankingCriteria
Top 20 students (all classes)1st total answers · 2nd distinct days with participation · 3rd accepted answers (no ❌)
Top 10 classesAnswers per enrolled student, so small classes compete on equal terms. Also shows the total and how many students took part.
Top 3 in each classSame criteria as the student top list, within each class.

💡 To climb the ranking: taking part a little every day is worth more than a lot on a single day, since days with participation break ties. Answering well helps too.

Example calculation

ClassStudentsAnswersPer studentPosition
Tuesday 7pm Class69015.0🥇 1st
Saturday Class1213211.0🥈 2nd

The Saturday class has more answers in total, but the Tuesday class comes first because each student took part more.

How an admin asks for the ranking

  • /ranking: last 6 months (default). /ranking 2: last 2 months (from 1 to 24).
  • The PDF includes each student's saved name and number, the total, the active days and the class. That's why it stays with the admins only.
  • The class name in the ranking is the WhatsApp group name.

8Class level and audio

Class level: !level

Each group has a level, which calibrates the definitions, examples, corrections and challenge questions. The word itself can be at any level.

LevelDoes Portuguese appear?
A1-A2 · A2-B1Yes: the 🇧🇷 line on the card and in the list, and the challenge explanation in Portuguese before the English.
B1-B2 · B2-C1 · C1-C2 · C2No: everything in English.
  • !level: anyone can see the class level.
  • !level B1-B2: only the teacher can change it.

Audio pronunciation

  • Each new word comes with two voice notes: the word and the example sentence.
  • In the weekly challenge, /2 reads the question out loud.

Answering by voice

Prefer to speak? Record a voice note replying to (quoting) a bot message. It transcribes what you said and treats it like a written message. Great for speaking practice.

Ana↩ replying to "🔁 1/5 · stall" 🎤 0:06
bot🎙️ my english stalled when I stopped watching series
bot👏 Ana answered: ✅ Great sentence! ...

Voice notes up to 60 seconds. A voice note that doesn't reply to a bot message is ignored, like any group conversation. The bot transcribes exactly what it heard, without correcting it.

How to add the bot to a new class

  1. A WhatsApp group admin adds the bot's number to the group.
  2. A bot admin sends in the group: !activate. The bot uses the WhatsApp group name as the class name.
  3. The bot confirms and the class can start using it: !help shows the commands.
  4. Set the level: !level A2-B1. Mark the teachers with !teacher @person (or /teachers NUMBER in private chat).

If nobody sends !activate within 24 hours, the bot leaves the group on its own, without sending a message.

11Limits and FAQ

Usage limits

To keep the bot fast and free during the trial, it has a few limits. When a limit is reached, the bot warns once and stops replying until the limit resets.

LimitValueBot's notice
Messages per person per minute6⏳ Easy there! I can't keep up that fast...
Messages per person per day150⏳ You've reached today's limit...
Messages per group per day400⏳ This group has reached today's limit...
Message length500 charactersThe bot asks for a shorter message
Audio per person per day10 minutes (60 s per voice note)🎙️ You've used up today's voice-message time...

The class's normal conversation doesn't count toward these limits: only what's meant for the bot.

🚀 To make the most of the 2 months:

  • Every new word from class becomes an @bot word in the group.
  • Answer the 19:00 review, even if it's just one word a day.
  • On Friday, keep an eye on the challenge: when the bot tags you, the class waits for your answer.
  • Write sentences about your own life: the bot corrects them and you learn to really use the word.
